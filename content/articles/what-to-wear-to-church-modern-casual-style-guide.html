Frequently asked questions

Can you wear jeans and a t-shirt or hoodie to church?

At most contemporary, non-denominational, and community churches, yes—clean jeans paired with a heavyweight graphic tee, crewneck, or hoodie is standard Sunday attire.

Should you take off a hat inside a church service?

For coffee lobbies, youth nights, and outdoor fellowship, caps are common everywhere; inside traditional sanctuaries (and during prayer), removing a cap is still a thoughtful sign of respect.
