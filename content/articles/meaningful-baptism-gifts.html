How to choose

  1. Start with the verse. If they shared a verse at their baptism, find a piece that matches it.
  2. Pick what they'll wear. Hats and phone cases avoid sizing guesswork entirely.
  3. Order early. Production typically takes 2–7 business days before shipping, so allow a week or two for the baptism date.
