Frequently asked questions

Where can I find a Philippians 4:13 shirt?

ArmorNGlory makes Philippians 4:13 ("I can do all things through Christ who strengthens me") apparel and accessories. See the Philippians 4:13 section above, or search the store.

What does Romans 8:37 "More Than Conquerors" mean?

Romans 8:37 says that in all things we are more than conquerors through Christ who loved us: victory isn't the absence of hardship but God's love holding through it. It's a favorite verse for athletes, people facing a hard season, and anyone who needs a reminder of where their strength comes from.
